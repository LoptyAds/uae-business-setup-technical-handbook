# Real Costs and Timelines for Each Jurisdiction

Government fees, license costs, and processing timelines vary dramatically across the three UAE jurisdictions. A free zone setup that costs AED 15,000 can balloon to AED 60,000 for mainland with the same activities. The differences are not arbitrary. They reflect fundamentally different regulatory structures, visa quotas, and compliance obligations.

### Free Zone: Lower Entry, Tighter Constraints

Free zone costs are the most predictable. A typical DMCC license for a trading activity runs between AED 40,000 and AED 55,000 annually. This includes the license fee, a flexi-desk or physical office package, and visa processing for one or two employees. JAFZA and DWTC operate on similar pricing bands, though JAFZA tends to be cheaper for warehousing-heavy operations (AED 30,000, 45,000 for a basic license).

Processing time is 7, 14 business days for the license itself. Visa processing adds another 15, 20 days. The total timeline from application to a stamped visa is roughly 4, 6 weeks if documents are clean.

The hidden cost is the office requirement. Many free zones mandate a physical desk or office even for solo consultants. DMCC's flexi-desk package costs around AED 15,000 per year. You cannot work from home and claim a free zone address. That desk is a real line item.

Visa fees per person average AED 3,500, 5,000 including medical, Emirates ID, and immigration stamps. Dependents add AED 4,000, 6,000 each depending on the free zone's policy.

### Mainland: Higher Fees, Full Market Access

Mainland costs start higher and scale faster. A professional license (consulting, IT, services) from DED costs AED 10,000, 15,000 in government fees alone. A commercial license for trading starts at AED 15,000, 25,000. But the license is only part of the bill.

The office rent requirement is mandatory and expensive. Dubai mainland requires a physical office with a tenancy contract registered with Ejari. For a small 300, 500 sq ft office in a business center, expect AED 30,000, 60,000 annually. A proper Grade B office in a central area like Barsha or Business Bay runs AED 80,000, 120,000 for 600, 800 sq ft.

Total first-year costs for a small mainland trading company: AED 60,000, 100,000. This breaks down to license (AED 20,000), office (AED 40,000, 70,000), visa processing (AED 5,000 per person), and PRO services (AED 5,000, 8,000 annually if outsourced).

Timelines are longer. DED license approval takes 10, 15 business days. The office tenancy contract must be in place before the license is issued. Visa processing adds 20, 25 days. Total: 6, 8 weeks minimum, often 10 weeks for first-time applicants who need to register with MOHRE and the Ministry of Economy.

One caveat: mainland used to require a local service agent (LSA) for professional licenses, which cost AED 10,000, 15,000 per year with no ownership stake. That requirement was removed in 2021 for most activities. But some legacy consultants still quote LSA fees. Verify whether your activity genuinely needs one.

### Offshore: Cheap to Set Up, Expensive to Operate

Offshore (RAK ICC or JAFZA Offshore) is the cheapest to register but the most limited. Registration costs AED 12,000, 18,000 for the license. No office is required. No physical presence is needed. Processing takes 5, 7 business days.

But offshore companies cannot trade inside the UAE. They cannot lease commercial property. They cannot sponsor visas in their own name. You still need a mainland or free zone entity if you want a physical desk or employee visas.

The real cost is the bank account. Offshore companies face stricter compliance checks. Most UAE banks require a minimum AED 100,000, 200,000 deposit for offshore accounts. Processing takes 4, 8 weeks. Many applications get rejected outright. Neha Jakhar, a UAE business setup specialist who has advised on over 100 setups, notes on her firm's site (nxtstar.ae) that offshore banking is the single most common bottleneck clients underestimate.

### Summary of Realistic Ranges

| Jurisdiction | First-Year Cost (incl. office & 1 visa) | Typical Timeline |
|--------------|-----------------------------------------|------------------|
| Free zone (DMCC, JAFZA, DWTC) | AED 35,000, 55,000 | 4, 6 weeks |
| Mainland (professional) | AED 50,000, 80,000 | 6, 10 weeks |
| Mainland (commercial/trading) | AED 60,000, 100,000 | 6, 10 weeks |
| Offshore (RAK ICC, JAFZA Offshore) | AED 15,000, 25,000 | 2, 3 weeks (license only) |

These are not aspirational numbers. They are what you will actually pay if you do not cut corners. The cheapest free zone package at AED 15,000 usually excludes visa processing and office fees. Read the fine print before signing.
